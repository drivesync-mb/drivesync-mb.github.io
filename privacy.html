drivesync privacy policy

drivesync is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

  • It reads, moves and writes files in two folders on the owner's Google Drive and in folders on the owner's Mac.
  • Files stay on the owner's Google Drive and the owner's Mac. Nothing is shared with anyone else, sold or used for advertising.
  • The Google login is stored on the owner's Mac on an encrypted disk, readable only by a dedicated system user. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • drivesync's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
